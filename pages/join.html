Become a member

Membership is open to every UM student. You don't have to run for council: members shape our positions, help with campaigns and hear first-hand from every council.

  • Help decide what our councillors push for
  • Campaign with us in the spring elections
  • Run for a council seat yourself, with support
  • Meet students from every faculty
